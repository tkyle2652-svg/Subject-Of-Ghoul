Scout handbook

Frequently asked questions

01What is Attack on Titan Revolution?

Attack on Titan Revolution is an action RPG experience on Roblox inspired by the Attack on Titan anime. Visit the official game hub for more details.

02Is it free to play?

Yes, the game is free to play. If you encounter a copy of this game which you pay to play, please report it to the official game support team.

03How do I get codes?

Codes and their availability can change over time. Check official AOTR announcements and the game's community channels for current codes or our discord server.

04How do I become a Titan Shifter?

You can become a Titan Shifter by getting a Titan serum which can be obtained by playing raids or aberrant missions.

05What are Families?

Families are part of AOTR's character use in progression. The rare families have unique stats and buffs to your journey. Their effects and availability may change, so check the current in-game information.

06What platforms can I play on?

This game is available across multiple devices. Check the Roblox listing and your device's compatibility for the latest platform support.

07Is there any benefit to joining the group?

Join Subject of Ghoul to find other players, coordinate group activities, and share tips with the community.

08Where can I find answers to more specific questions?

Start with the official AOTR website and game community discord server. You can also ask fellow scouts in our regiment discord server.
